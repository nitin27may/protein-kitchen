---
title: Pressing and marinating tofu
tags: [tofu, prep]
---

Tofu holds a lot of water. Water dilutes flavour, steams instead of browning, and makes baked tofu rubbery. Pressing fixes that. Super-firm tofu, which is vacuum-packed with little water, can skip this step.

## Steps

1. Drain the block and wrap it in a clean tea towel or a few layers of paper towel.
2. Put it on a plate, set a cutting board on top, and weigh it down with two or three cans or a heavy pan.
3. Press for 15 to 30 minutes. The block will lose about a quarter of its thickness.
4. Cut into cubes, slabs or crumbles as the recipe says.
5. To marinate, toss the tofu with oil, an acid (lemon juice or vinegar) and salt. Pressed tofu absorbs marinade better, so 30 minutes is enough. Overnight in the fridge gives a stronger flavour.

## Common mistakes

- Pressing too hard or too fast. The block cracks. Use light, steady weight.
- Marinating unpressed tofu. The marinade runs off and the cubes stay bland.
- Skipping salt in the marinade. Tofu is mild and needs seasoning that reaches the inside.
- Crowding the pan or tray when cooking. Leave space between pieces so steam escapes and the edges crisp.

Tofu keeps in the fridge for 3 to 4 days once opened, covered in fresh water that you change daily.
