---
title: Cooking dried and canned beans safely
tags: [legumes, prep]
---

Dried beans are cheap and high in protein, but they need correct cooking. Canned beans are a fine shortcut.

## Dried beans

1. Soak in plenty of cold water for 8 to 12 hours. Discard the soaking water, which carries some of the gas-causing sugars.
2. Rinse, cover with fresh water and cook. A pressure cooker takes 20 to 30 minutes for chickpeas and rajma. A pot takes 60 to 90 minutes.
3. Salt after the beans start to soften, or add it from the start. Both work. Beans should be creamy inside, not chalky.

## Safety

Red kidney beans and other kidney bean types contain a natural toxin that causes severe stomach upset if undercooked. After soaking, boil them vigorously for at least 10 minutes, then simmer until tender. Do not cook them only in a slow cooker or at low temperature.

## Canned beans

Drain and rinse. This removes much of the sodium and the starchy liquid. Use a small amount of the liquid for soups if you want body.

## Common mistakes

- Skipping the soak for kidney beans.
- Hard water or old beans. Both make beans slow to soften. Older beans may need longer.
- Adding lots of acid early. Tomatoes and vinegar slow softening, so add them once the beans are tender.

Cooked beans keep 4 days in the fridge in their cooking liquid, and freeze well for 3 months.
