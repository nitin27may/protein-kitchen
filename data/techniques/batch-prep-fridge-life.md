Cooking once and eating several times is what keeps a meal plan alive. These are guideline fridge times for food kept at 4°C or colder in sealed containers.

## Fridge life

| Item | Keeps |
|---|---|
| Dressings with fresh garlic or herbs | 5 to 7 days |
| Yoghurt dressings | 4 days |
| Cooked beans and lentils | 4 days |
| Cooked grains | 4 to 5 days |
| Soups and stews | 3 to 4 days |
| Cut raw vegetables | 3 to 4 days |
| Baked muffins and bars | 3 to 4 days |
| Opened tofu in fresh water | 3 to 4 days |

## Steps for a Sunday session

1. Start the longest task first: beans in the pressure cooker, grains on the stove.
2. While they cook, make dressings and chop vegetables.
3. Roast a tray of vegetables or chickpeas.
4. Cool everything fully before sealing. Hot food in a closed container sweats and spoils faster.
5. Label containers with the date.
6. Keep dressing, greens and wet items apart until serving.

## Common mistakes

- Dressing salads in advance. Leaves go limp.
- Leaving food out to cool for more than 2 hours.
- Reheating soups and beans only warm. Bring leftovers to a steaming boil.

Freeze what you will not eat within the fridge window. Cooked beans, soups and baked goods all freeze well.
