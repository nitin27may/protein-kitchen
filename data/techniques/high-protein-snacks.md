---
title: Building a fast high-protein snack
tags: [snack, meal-prep]
---

A snack that keeps you full has a protein anchor, something fresh or crunchy, and no cooking. Aim for 12 g of protein or more.

## The formula

1. **Anchor**: Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, paneer, edamame, tofu, chickpeas or a spoon of peanut butter.
2. **Fresh or crunchy**: cucumber, tomato, carrot, seeds, nuts, sprouts.
3. **Flavour**: lemon juice, chaat spice, cumin, salt, a little honey for sweet versions.

## Steps

1. Keep the anchors ready. Cooked chickpeas, cubed paneer and a tub of Greek yogurt cover most of the list.
2. Weigh the anchor first. The gram weights in the recipe are what deliver the protein.
3. Add the fresh or crunchy part and the flavour just before eating, so nothing goes soft.
4. Taste for salt and acid. A squeeze of lemon lifts almost every savoury snack.

## Zero-cook rules

- Dry cubed paneer or chickpeas on a towel before adding spice, otherwise the flavour slides off.
- Salt cottage cheese and tomato at the last moment. Salt draws water out and makes the bowl watery.
- Toast seeds and nuts in a dry pan for two minutes for much more flavour.

## Storage

Plain anchors keep in the fridge for the times in the batch prep card: cooked chickpeas for 4 days, yogurt-based mixes for 4 days. Dressed snacks with tomato or cucumber are best eaten the same day. Energy balls keep a week in a sealed container.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing a snack under 10 g of protein and wondering why you are hungry an hour later.
- Adding fruit and honey to every yogurt bowl. One spoon of sweet is enough.
- Skipping the crunch. Texture is what makes it feel like food.
