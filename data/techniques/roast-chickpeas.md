---
title: Crispy roasted chickpeas
tags: [chickpeas, snack, oven]
---

Roasted chickpeas turn soft and chewy when moisture stays trapped. Dryness is the whole technique.

## Steps

1. Drain canned chickpeas and rinse, or use home-cooked ones.
2. Pat dry with a towel, then spread on a clean towel for 10 to 15 minutes. Rub gently so loose skins come off. The skins are optional to remove.
3. Preheat the oven to 200°C (conventional) with the rack in the middle.
4. Toss with a little oil and salt in a bowl. Use about 1 teaspoon of oil per cup of chickpeas, so they crisp and do not fry.
5. Spread in a single layer on a rimmed tray.
6. Roast 25 to 35 minutes, shaking the tray halfway, until golden and they rattle on the tray.
7. Season with spices straight out of the oven. Spices burn if they go in early.

## Convection and air fryer

Reduce the oven to 185°C for convection. In an air fryer, use 185°C for about 15 to 20 minutes, shaking twice.

## Common mistakes

- Roasting damp chickpeas. They steam and stay soft.
- Crowding the tray.
- Adding spices at the start. Fine powders scorch.
- Storing warm. Cool completely before putting them in a container.

They are crispiest the day they are made. A loosely covered jar keeps them crunchy for 2 to 3 days; sealed tightly, they soften.
