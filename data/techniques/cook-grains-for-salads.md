---
title: Cooking grains for salads
tags: [grains, salad]
---

Grain salads fail when the grain is mushy, clumped or dressed hot. Cook it so the grains stay separate and cool it before dressing.

## Quinoa

1. Rinse in a fine sieve for 30 seconds to remove the bitter coating.
2. Use about two parts water to one part dry quinoa by volume, with a pinch of salt.
3. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on low for 12 to 15 minutes until the water is gone and you see tiny spirals.
4. Rest, covered and off the heat, for 5 minutes.
5. Fluff with a fork and spread on a tray to cool.

## Bulgur

Fine bulgur needs no boiling. Cover it with boiling water in equal volume, cover the bowl and leave for 15 to 20 minutes. Medium and coarse bulgur needs about 10 to 15 minutes of simmering. Drain any extra water and fluff.

## Couscous

Pour boiling water or hot stock over it in equal volume, cover for 5 minutes, then fluff.

## Common mistakes

- Dressing hot grain. It absorbs too much dressing and goes greasy.
- Overcooking. Taste a grain at the minimum time.
- Stirring during simmering. It releases starch and turns the pot gluey.
- Under-salting. Salt the cooking water, not just the finished salad.

Cooked grains keep 4 to 5 days in the fridge in a sealed container. Dry quinoa and bulgur are the version we weigh in recipes, so use the cooked-state figure only when a recipe says so.
