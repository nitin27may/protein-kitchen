---
title: Making soup that tastes like something
tags: [soup, stove]
---

Good soup builds flavour in layers. Most of it happens in the first ten minutes.

## Steps

1. Heat a little oil in a heavy pot. Cook chopped onion, carrot and celery gently for 8 to 10 minutes until soft and sweet. Do not brown them unless the recipe says so.
2. Add garlic, spices and tomato paste. Stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add the main ingredients (lentils, beans, vegetables) and enough water to cover by 2 to 3 cm.
4. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer. Cook until the lentils or vegetables are soft.
5. Season at the end. Add salt in stages, then acid (lemon juice or vinegar) just before serving. Acid brightens a flat soup.

## Blending safely

Hot soup expands in a closed blender. Fill the jug only half, vent the lid, cover with a towel and start on low. An immersion blender in the pot avoids the problem.

## Storage

Cool soup quickly and refrigerate within 2 hours. It keeps 3 to 4 days. Freeze in portions for up to 3 months. Soups with lentils and beans thicken as they sit, so add water when reheating.

## Common mistakes

- Boiling hard. It breaks up beans and clouds the broth.
- Salting only at the start. Flavour concentrates as water leaves.
- Skipping acid. It is the cheapest fix for a dull soup.
