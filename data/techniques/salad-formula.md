A salad that works follows a formula. A salad that lacks one part tastes flat or leaves you hungry.

## The formula

1. **Base**: leafy greens (romaine, arugula, spinach, kale) or a cooked grain (quinoa, bulgur).
2. **Protein**: beans, chickpeas, lentils, tofu, edamame, cottage cheese or paneer. Aim for 25 to 35 g of protein in a main-meal salad.
3. **Crunch**: raw vegetables, nuts or seeds, toasted chickpeas.
4. **Acid**: lemon juice, vinegar or yoghurt-based dressing.
5. **Fat**: olive oil, tahini, avocado, nuts.
6. **Salt and herbs**: season in layers, not just at the end.

## Steps

1. Cook or open the protein and cool it. Warm beans wilt greens.
2. Wash and dry the greens. Wet leaves dilute the dressing.
3. Dress hearty ingredients (beans, grains, kale) first and let them sit 10 minutes. They absorb flavour.
4. Add delicate greens and soft items last.
5. Taste and correct with salt and acid before serving.

## Common mistakes

- Dressing a delicate salad early. It turns soggy within minutes.
- Under-salting beans. They need seasoning more than lettuce does.
- Skipping the crunch. Texture is what makes a salad feel like a meal.
- Overdressing. Start with half and add.

For meal prep, keep dressing, greens and wet items in separate containers and combine at the table.
