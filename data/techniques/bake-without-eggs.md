---
title: Baking without eggs
tags: [baking, eggless]
---

A lacto-vegetarian kitchen has no eggs, so binding and lift come from other sources. Baking is chemistry, so weigh ingredients in grams.

## Egg replacements

- **Flax egg**: 1 tablespoon ground flaxseed plus 3 tablespoons water, stirred and rested for 5 minutes until gel-like. Replaces one egg in muffins, bars and pancakes. Use ground flax, not whole.
- **Yoghurt**: about 60 g of thick yoghurt replaces one egg in cakes and muffins and adds protein and moisture.
- **Aquafaba**: the liquid from canned chickpeas. Three tablespoons stand in for one egg, and it whips like egg white.
- **Besan**: chickpea flour with water works as a binder in savoury bakes.

## Steps

1. Weigh dry ingredients and mix them with the raising agent.
2. Prepare the replacement and mix the wet ingredients separately.
3. Combine the two gently, only until no dry flour shows. Overmixing makes tough bakes.
4. Rest the batter for 5 minutes, then bake at the stated temperature.
5. Test with a skewer. It should come out clean with a few moist crumbs.

## Common mistakes

- Measuring flour by cup. Packed flour can weigh 20 percent more.
- Opening the oven early. The bake sinks.
- Expecting the same rise as an egg bake. Eggless bakes are denser and moister.

Cool bakes on a rack for 10 minutes. They keep 3 to 4 days in the fridge in a sealed container.
