Most roasting and baking problems come from the oven, not the recipe. All temperatures here are in Celsius.

## Steps

1. Preheat for at least 15 minutes. An oven that beeps at temperature is often still cool in the walls and on the rack.
2. Use the middle rack unless the recipe says otherwise. Top racks burn the surface, bottom racks burn the base.
3. Use a rimmed metal sheet pan. Line it with parchment so nothing sticks.
4. Cut vegetables to a similar size so they finish together.
5. Leave space between pieces. Crowded pans steam and turn soft instead of browning.
6. Turn or shake the pan halfway through.

## Convection and air fryer

- Convection (fan) ovens cook faster. Reduce the temperature by 15 degrees from a conventional recipe, and check a few minutes early.
- An air fryer behaves like a small, strong convection oven. Reduce the temperature by about 15 degrees and the time by about 20 percent, and work in a single layer.

## Common mistakes

- Opening the door repeatedly. Each opening drops the temperature.
- Using a glass or dark pan without adjusting. Glass holds heat and can over-brown the base.
- Oiling too little. A tablespoon of oil per tray helps browning and flavour.
- Trusting the clock only. Look at colour and test with a fork.

Oven thermometers are cheap. If your food always cooks faster or slower than the recipe, the dial is probably off.
